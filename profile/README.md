### [Gruffet](https://github.com/Zqtion/Gruffet) — Lead discovery & competitor intelligence

**Find businesses. Verify contacts. Research competitors. Keep the evidence.**

Gruffet is a free, open-source Python CLI for local-business discovery and competitor research with AI coding agents. It brings together public website investigation, evidence-oriented verification checks, and structured Excel exports.
